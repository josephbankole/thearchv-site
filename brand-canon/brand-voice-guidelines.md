## 1. Brand Snapshot

**Who we are:** A faceless football culture page built on nostalgia, debate, and archive-grade football knowledge. We are not a news account — we are the memory and the argument of football.

**Mission:** Create shareable nostalgia, comment-inducing debates, and save-worthy football knowledge.

**Channels:**
- Instagram (primary): `@thearchvfc` — business profile (rebranded from `@thearchvfc`, 2026-07-02)
- Twitter/X: `@thearchvfc`
- YouTube: `thearchvca`

**Positioning line:** *Football has a memory. We are the archive.*

**Why we win:** Most football pages chase results, clips, and team news. We chase *stories* — because people follow stories, not fixtures.

## 2. Voice Attributes — We Are / We Are Not

| WE ARE | WE ARE NOT |
|---|---|
| The knowledgeable mate who remembers everything | A stats robot reciting Opta lines |
| Nostalgic and warm about the past | Bitter "football was better back then" gatekeepers |
| Confident and authoritative on tactics/history | Arrogant or condescending to casual fans |
| Provocative — we start the argument | The ones who end the argument (we never give our verdict first) |
| Neutral referees in debates | Picking sides and alienating half the audience |
| Punchy, scroll-stopping, few words | Wordy, academic, paragraph-heavy |
| Emotional about football moments | Cynical or detached |
| Inclusive of all eras and all fanbases | Tribal or club-biased |

## 4. Vocabulary & Terminology

**Use:**
- "Only real ones remember…"
- "Prime [player]" (Prime Neymar, Prime Ronaldinho)
- "Forgotten" / "Wonderkid" / "Cult hero"
- "Broken" (for overpowered FIFA cards / dominant players)
- "Peaked" ("Football peaked here")
- "The archive" / "Then vs Now"
- "Agree or disagree?" / "Who you got?" / "Settle it"

**Avoid:**
- Clickbait that doesn't pay off (the warning in the thesis: no low-effort AI slop)
- Betting odds language on non-betting posts
- Insider jargon with no explanation for casual fans
- Declaring winners in debates
- Negative gatekeeping ("you wouldn't understand")

**Numbers & names:** Always verify stats, dates, transfer fees, and trophy counts before posting. Accuracy is the brand's credibility moat — one wrong stat in a knowledge account costs trust.

## 7. Daily Posting System

**6 posts per day, IG + selected X cross-posts.** The engine generates 10 ideas, scores each for virality (§22), and schedules the top 6. The other 4 are logged as unused ideas — they can be revisited if they score higher on a future run with better trend relevance.

## 8. Caption & Copy Rules

**Hook (first line) — must stop the scroll. Big, few words.**
- "Football peaked here."
- "You'll never see this again."
- "This player was unfair."
- "Only real ones remember this."

**Structure for captions:**
1. Hook line (the same energy as the slide-1 headline)
2. 1–2 lines of context or the memory trigger
3. Clear CTA question that forces a comment

**CTA patterns (always end on one):**
- "Who was your favourite forgotten player?"
- "Agree or disagree?"
- "Who you got — and why?"
- "Tag someone who remembers."

**Reel formula:**
- 0–2s: strong hook
- 3–10s: visual proof
- 10–20s: the story
- 20–30s: the question

## 13. Growth System v2 — engagement loops, series, FPR (added 2026-06-02)

Informed by a competitor teardown ("Alternative Football Universe"). We adopt the *mechanics*, not the skin — our palette, corner-bracket frame, and voice are unchanged.

### North-star metric: FPR (Followers per 1,000 Reach)
Judge every post by **FPR = (followers gained ÷ reach) × 1000**, not raw likes. A post at 50k reach / 1,000 follows (FPR 20) beats 500k reach / 100 follows (FPR 0.2). After ~2–3 weeks of data: cut the bottom-FPR formats, reallocate to the top-FPR engines. Track shares as the secondary signal.

**The engine generates 10 ideas and publishes the top 6 by virality score (§22).** Virality scores are recalibrated daily from actual past results — the scoring system improves every run.

### The 4 loops — every post triggers at least one
- **SHARE** (tribal/tag): "Send this to the mate who still defends him." · "Tag who remembers."
- **SAVE** (utility): checklists, projected XIs, record lists, forecasts. "Save this projected 2026 XI."
- **COMMENT** (friction): neutral debate / ranking / tier bait. "Tell us why we're wrong 👇"
- **FOLLOW** (series hook): "Part N of [series]. Tomorrow: [tease]. Follow @thearchvfc."

### Series continuity
Number recurring posts ("Part 14 — Archive Debate"), end with a next-up tease, and close every caption with the unified anchor line (2026-07-03): **"Follow @thearchvfc for your daily football and archive. Visit thearchv.ca/start for more."**

### New franchises (Higgsfield-powered)
- **"What If…" / Alternate Timeline** (high-share flagship) — split real-history vs AI-generated alternate, labelled **"⇄ ALTERNATE TIMELINE / AI"** on-image. Always framed as speculative fiction so it never misleads (e.g. *What if Neymar wasn't injured in 2014?*).
- **"FUT Legends"** — FIFA 12–15 overpowered cards (Doumbia, Ibarbo, El Shaarawy…), card-style layout + ratings; gaming-nostalgia save/share bait.
- **"The Day Football Changed"** — cinematic turning-point Reels (7–1, Zidane red card, Agüero 93:20).

### One event → multi-asset newsjacking
When news breaks, spin one event into up to four angles: (1) WC2026 impact, (2) stat/metric split vs a legend, (3) FUT/gaming rewind, (4) a What-If. Ship the strongest 1–2 the same day.

### WC2026-window pillar mix (supersedes §6 until the tournament)
Nostalgia 25% (incl. FUT Legends) · What-If / Alternate 20% · World Cup 2026 20% · Debate/Comparison 15% · Newsjacking 10% · Stat/Tactical utility (save-bait) 5% · Community Pick 5% (Sunday Stories vote).

### X (Twitter) track
Repurpose the day's strongest 3–4 posts as X posts — text hook + the image — with a **bookmark prompt** ("Bookmark this thread") and a **reply-matrix** question. Manual growth (not automated): ~10 thoughtful replies/day under big football accounts within minutes of their posts.

## 14. Instagram Reels system (updated 2026-06-05)

Reels are the #1 discovery/growth lever on Instagram — they're shown to non-followers. Run **1 strong Reel every Friday** (quality over frequency; weekly cadence).

**Cadence: Fridays only.** The engine does NOT generate a Reel on non-Friday runs. On Friday runs, Step 5 is: research → propose idea → deliver the formatted script to the user for review. The user approves, then the Higgsfield generation happens separately (or the user produces it manually).

---

### Friday Reel workflow — research & propose (engine Step 5 on Fridays)

1. **Research:** find the most compelling "On This Day" match that falls on the exact Friday date (±1 day acceptable if the exact date has nothing notable). Verify match was played on that date against ≥2 sources (RSSSF / FIFA / Wikipedia / official). Prioritise: World Cup match > major international final > iconic UCL/league moment. Confirm scoreline, scorers, venue, competition, and date.

2. **Present-day bridge:** identify the strongest link between that match and what's happening in football *right now* (upcoming tournament, anniversary, host nation angle, player's legacy, etc.).

3. **Output the formatted reel proposal** — do not generate Higgsfield assets; deliver the script for user review. Format:

```
thearchvfc REEL — [Match Name, Year]

1. HOOK — "[One punchy sentence. The drama, immediately.]"
2. CONTEXT — "[Tournament. Stage. The setting in one line.]"
3. STAKES — "[Who was involved. What was expected. Why it mattered.]"
4. PROBLEM/TENSION — "[The thing nobody saw coming, or the obstacle.]"
5. PAYOFF — "[What actually happened. The result. The moment.]"
6. SIGNIFICANCE — "[Why this is remembered. What it meant for football/history.]"
7. PRESENT-DAY BRIDGE — "[The link to now — tournament, anniversary, host nation, player legacy.]"
8. EMOTIONAL CLOSING — "[One resonant line. Leave them feeling something.]"
9. SAVE CTA — "[Specific, relevant. Not generic 'save this'.]"
10. SERIES CTA — "Match of the Day — On This Day. Part [N]."
11. FOLLOW CTA — "Follow for your daily football archive."
```

4. **After user approves** the script, Higgsfield generation proceeds (next session or same session if user confirms):
   - **Default pipeline — stills + ffmpeg motion (~12 credits total).** Generate 5–6 stills via `nano_banana_pro` (4:5, ~2 cr each), then animate with the ffmpeg engine (parallax push, zoom cuts, whip-pans, kinetic captions, grain, VO mix). Camera-driven motion is enough — subject motion not required.
   - **Upgrade (when a subject needs to visibly move):** 1–2 hero shots via **Veo 3.1 Lite** (8 cr/8s). Rest stay as animated stills. Call `get_cost` first. See §23 model ladder.
   - `nano_banana_pro` → hero **match infographic** still (framing + scoreboard layout).
   - **Voice:** British male RP on odd Fridays, British female RP on even Fridays (by calendar date). Burn in captions via ffmpeg — do NOT render text in the video generation.
   - Schedule to Buffer as IG Reel (`notification` mode) + X cross-post (`automatic`)

**Reel metrics:** judge by **watch-through rate + shares/saves first**, then FPR.

## 16A. LinkedIn carousel system (added 2026-06-06, from user-supplied teardown)

Carousels become a **core Company Page format** — they build the brand on the profile, deliver save-worthy value, and feed the site/store funnel (thearchv.ca + the Etsy store; newsletter killed D78). Principles adapted from a high-performing carousel teardown (5.1k likes / 4.5k shares), re-skinned to the thearchvfc identity.

### The 7 rules (apply to every carousel)
1. **The cover has one job: stop the scroll.** Not explain, not introduce. Anton condensed, high contrast, **≤8 words**, always a number or a promise ("£1bn. One court case." / "Three managers. One fatal org chart."). If the cover needs more than 8 words, the hook is wrong.
2. **Slide 2 confirms the hook or they leave.** The cover gets the swipe; slide 2 earns the rest — state the problem and the stakes immediately. No throat-clearing, no "in this carousel we'll cover".
3. **Every slide works as a standalone screenshot.** One idea per slide. If a slide sent without context still lands, it's right. Max two fonts (Anton + Inter — already locked). Clutter kills engagement.
4. **One designated SAVE slide per carousel.** A framework, checklist or fill-in-the-blank sharp enough to be quoted ("The Sacchi Test: 4 questions for your operating model"). This is the slide that turns reach into saves — if no single slide is save-worthy, the carousel isn't.
5. **AI drafts the architecture, the brand handles the taste.** The engine generates the slide-by-slide structure from the IP-bank beat arcs; the §16 executive register and the two-source accuracy pass are applied on top. Never ship the raw draft.
6. **The caption is the second post; the CTA is the engine.** Restate the core idea natively (~60% rule unchanged), add one comment trigger, close with the site/store CTA (thearchv.ca, or the Etsy store for poster/merch posts). One carousel, three wins: follow + save + click. No newsletter/subscribe CTA (D78).
7. **Penultimate slide = thesis restatement, final slide = CTA.** Mirror the cover's energy on the close ("Football's history isn't trivia. It's case law."), then the funnel slide: thearchv.ca tease (or "shop the prints" for the poster line) + badge.

### Slide anatomy (thearchvfc skin)
- **Header strip on every slide:** `JUN ©2026 · thearchvfc · THE DAILY ARCHIVE` — small Inter caps, gold, top edge inside the safe area. Reads as a publication masthead, not a post.
- **Numbered gold seal badge** (1, 2, 3…) top-left of each content slide for swipe momentum; **slide counter** implicit via the deck.
- **Background rhythm:** alternate navy `#0C2A3E` → cream `#F2EAD3` → one green `#2E6B3A` or textured accent slide mid-deck to reset attention (extends §10 carousel rhythm — make it standard for carousels).
- Corner brackets, film grain, badge bottom-right, §16 premium-editorial restraint (diagram/timeline energy, never IG hype). All text inside the inner 86% safe area (§16C sizing rules).
- **Length 7–9 slides**, 4:5 portrait (1080×1350).

## 16C. IG image sizing & safe-area — STOP text being cropped (added 2026-06-04, user-directed)

**Problem observed:** feed stills were rendered **9:16 (1080×1920)** but published as Instagram **feed photos** (`type:"post"`, `shouldShareToFeed:true`). Instagram crops feed images to a **maximum 4:5 portrait**, so a taller 9:16 frame gets **centre-cropped** — chopping the top headline and the bottom `@thearchvfc` handle/badge. That's the "words get cut off."

**Fix (supersedes SKILL.md step 5 aspect_ratio for FEED STILLS):**
- **Feed stills → generate at `aspect_ratio "4:5"` (1080×1350), 2k.** What you design is exactly what shows in-feed — no crop. (1:1 is an acceptable fallback if 4:5 is unavailable.)
- **Reels stay `9:16` (1080×1920) full-frame** — Reels play full-screen and are *not* cropped, so the existing 9:16 is correct for video only.
- **Safe-area rule (every still):** keep the headline, all key text, and the handle+badge inside the **inner ~86%** of the frame — leave **≥7% padding on every edge**, and never place text in the outer ~10% top or bottom. Build prompts so the headline sits in the central band, badge bottom-right but inset from the corner.
- **Verification step (do this before scheduling each still):** open the rendered image and confirm the **full headline and the @thearchvfc handle are inside the frame with margin** — nothing clipped at any edge. If clipped, regenerate with more padding. Add this check to the run's verification pass.
- Keep using `results.minUrl` for Buffer (<8 MB). Composite/real-photo United route (§16B) also renders at 4:5 for feed.
- **United Transfer Desk IG feed graphics MUST be 4:5 too.** Audit of 2026-06-04 found the 3 United IG posts published as **16:9 landscape** (1024×576, 768×432, 1672×941) — the §15 announcement poster (16:9) is **X-only**; for the IG feed render or re-crop a separate **4:5 portrait** version. - **David Ornstein** — breaks it plainly and calmly. Front-load the news in the first sentence, then the key facts (fee, contract length, status, what's next). Short declarative sentences. No adjectives doing hype work. Authority comes from precision and sourcing, not volume.

Never imply done when it isn't.
- **Specifics with caveats:** "a fee in the region of £35m plus add-ons", "a four-year contract with the option of a further year", "a medical is scheduled", "personal terms are agreed", "the clubs are still negotiating the structure of the fee".

## 22. Virality Scoring Framework (added 2026-06-05, user-directed)

Every engine run generates **10 post ideas** and schedules the **top 6 by virality score**. This section defines how to score, log, and improve those scores over time.

---

### A. Scoring criteria and weights (starting weights — recalibrated daily per §22D)

Score each idea 0–10 using the weighted criteria below. Multiply each raw score (0–10) by its weight, sum the products, round to one decimal place.

| # | Criterion | Weight | What to measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook strength** | 0.25 | Does the hook create an instant, irresistible reason to stop scrolling? (10 = unmissable claim/fact/question; 1 = vague/generic) |
| 2 | **Loop type triggered** | 0.20 | Does the format lock in a completion/share/save loop? Save-bait = 9–10; debate = 8–9; nostalgia hook = 7–9; standard recap = 4–6; no loop = 1–3 |
| 3 | **Trend / moment relevance** | 0.20 | How closely does it tie to what football Twitter/IG is talking about *today*? (today's trending topic = 10; evergreen with no hook = 4; stale = 1) |
| 4 | **Series continuity** | 0.10 | Does it extend a proven franchise (Match of the Day, United Transfer Desk, WC2026 countdown)? Yes + strong fit = 8–10; no = 5 baseline |
| 5 | **Format FPR history** | 0.15 | What FPR does this format historically produce? Use performance-log data. No data yet = 5 (neutral). Calibrate from actual runs. |
| 6 | **Shareability** | 0.10 | Would a football fan share/repost without prompting? (stat nobody knows + "save for later" = 9–10; divisive debate = 8–9; niche topic = 4–6) |

**Score formula:**  
`Virality Score = (Hook × 0.25) + (Loop × 0.20) + (Trend × 0.20) + (Series × 0.10) + (FPR_history × 0.15) + (Share × 0.10)`

Max possible = 10.0.

---

### B. How to apply the scores each run

1. **Generate 10 ideas** with headline, pillar, and format type noted.
2. **Score each idea** across all 6 criteria. Show the score breakdown as a table in the run summary (ideas ranked 1–10 by total score).
3. **Schedule slots 1–6** (highest score fills the 08:00 slot; sixth-highest fills 17:00 — or the Reel slot on Fridays). The 4 unused ideas are logged with their scores.
4. **Log predicted scores in performance-log.md** alongside the post ID, slot, and format — one row per post. The actual FPR column is filled in at the next run once the user provides analytics data.

**Performance log row format (extend existing schema):**
```
| Date | Post ID | Slot | Format | Pillar | Predicted Score | Actual FPR | Notes |
```

---

### C. Worked example

Idea: *"The save Buffon made in 2003 that nobody talks about — and why it changed the final"*

| Criterion | Raw (0–10) | Weight | Weighted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook strength | 9 (immediate curiosity gap) | 0.25 | 2.25 |
| Loop type | 8 (save-bait + nostalgia) | 0.20 | 1.60 |
| Trend relevance | 6 (no active news hook) | 0.20 | 1.20 |
| Series continuity | 7 (fits Match of the Day) | 0.10 | 0.70 |
| Format FPR history | 7 (nostalgia stills perform well) | 0.15 | 1.05 |
| Shareability | 8 (stat nobody knows) | 0.10 | 0.80 |
| **Total** | | | **7.6** |

---

### D. Daily recalibration — how the scoring improves each run

After ≥3 actual FPR data points exist for a given format or criterion pattern, the engine adjusts weights. Rules:

1. **Read the performance log** at Step 1. Identify which format/pillar combinations have the most data.
2. **Compare predicted score vs actual FPR** for posts where both are logged. If a criterion is consistently over- or under-predicting (i.e., high-score posts underperform, or low-score posts overperform), adjust that criterion's weight up or down by 0.02–0.05 per run, capped at the limits below.
3. **Weight bounds** — no single criterion can go below 0.05 or above 0.35. Total weights must always sum to 1.00.
4. **Log the updated weights** in the run summary note. Example: `"Recalibration: Trend weight ↑ 0.02 → 0.22 (nostalgia posts outperforming predicted; trend signal stronger than expected)."` This note becomes the new starting weights for the next run.
5. **Minimum data threshold:** do not adjust weights unless at least **3 confirmed actual FPR data points** back the change. Before that threshold, keep starting weights and note "insufficient data — holding weights."

**Current weights (starting — update this line each time weights change):**
Hook 0.25 · Loop 0.20 · Trend 0.20 · Series 0.10 · FPR_history 0.15 · Share 0.10

---

### E. Scoring guard — tie-breaking and edge cases

- **Tie on total score:** prefer the idea that covers an under-represented pillar in the day's mix (§13 WC-window ratios).
- **Transfer news:** always scores at least 8.0 on trend relevance when it is today's live story — bump to 10 if it's a confirmed signing or major leak. Transfer news can displace a lower-scoring scheduled idea.
- **Friday Reel slot:** the Reel fills the 17:00 slot regardless of score (it is a guaranteed placement). Score the Reel idea for the record, but do not let a higher-scoring feed still displace it.
- **Unverified stats:** an idea whose central claim cannot be verified in ≤2 sources is disqualified entirely and replaced with the next-ranked idea from the 10. Log the disqualification.

## 23. Higgsfield model ladder & credit rules (added 2026-06-05)

Cost-optimised defaults from the June 2026 spend analysis. Seedance 2.0 ran 87% of spend (553 of 634 credits) — learning-curve re-rolls inflated it, but the structural fix is using the right model for each job.

### Model ladder (cheapest → most capable)

| Tier | Model | Rate | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stills** (default) | `nano_banana_pro` | ~2 cr/image | Feed stills, carousel frames, match infographics, portrait refs |
| **Animated stills** (default Reel) | nano_banana_pro + ffmpeg | ~2–12 cr total | Full Reel via parallax push, whip-pans, kinetic captions — proven on the verdict Reel |
| **Light video** | Veo 3.1 Lite | 8 cr/8s (~1 cr/s) | 1–2 hero shots needing real subject motion; 4.5× cheaper than Seedance |
| **Heavy video** (last resort) | Seedance 2.0 720p | 36 cr/8s (~4.5 cr/s) | Rare hero shots only; call `get_cost` first; explicit user sign-off on spend |

### Rules

1. **Stills + ffmpeg motion is the default for Reels.** Generate 5–6 stills at 4:5 (`nano_banana_pro`, ~2 cr each = ~12 total), then animate with the ffmpeg pipeline (parallax push, zoom, whip-pans, grain, kinetic captions). Subject motion is not required — camera motion is enough.
2. **When a subject truly needs to move, use Veo 3.1 Lite.** Reserve for 1–2 hero shots maximum per Reel. Do not use Seedance as the default video model.
3. **Seedance is a last resort.** Only when neither stills+ffmpeg nor Veo Lite can deliver the needed motion. Always call `get_cost` before any Seedance batch. Log each Seedance job in the run summary with justification.
4. **`get_cost` before every batch.** Before generating more than 2 video clips, check projected cost and surface it in the run summary.
5. **Minimum duration.** Generate at the minimum duration needed — loop or hold-freeze in ffmpeg rather than rendering extra seconds in Higgsfield.
6. **Resolution tiering.** Render `2k`/high for hero/foreground shots; `480p` is acceptable for background/texture/grain shots to save credits.
7. **Text-free prompts.** All video and image prompts must explicitly request no on-screen text in the generation. Burn captions in post via ffmpeg. This prevents NSFW/text-gibberish re-rolls.
8. **No aerial / bird's-eye angle.** Avoids NSFW false positives on crowd/stadium shots. Use close-up, medium, or low-angle player shots.

### Cost benchmarks (8s Reel, 6 shots)

| Pipeline | Cost |
|---|---|
| Stills + ffmpeg only | ~12 cr |
| Stills + ffmpeg + 1 Veo Lite hero shot | ~20 cr |
| Stills + ffmpeg + 2 Veo Lite hero shots | ~28 cr |
| Full Seedance 720p (6 clips) | ~216 cr |
