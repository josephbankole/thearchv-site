# The ARCHV Wire: design inputs (2026-09-26)

Founder decisions, 2026-09-26 (binding):
1. A DAILY Wire: one top article per permitted free source per day. Not live, not a firehose.
2. Only sources whose terms permit a headline and link on a third-party site; drop the rest and say why.
3. Each item: the headline verbatim, the source name as a text label, the publish time, and a one-line ARCHV note on why it matters. Tap opens the publisher's article. No publisher thumbnails, no publisher logos, no article body stored or shown.
4. Daily tables and fixtures as native components: Premier League table, Manchester United fixtures and results, F1 standings and next race, and other sports only where a clean free source exists.
5. Every app tab: football (Manchester United plus general), NFL, F1, tennis, golf, basketball (NBA and WNBA equally).
6. ARCHV's own writing drops to 2 or 3 pieces a week, the Substack pieces. Home leads with the latest long read, then the Wire, then tables.
7. Calm brand: no tickers, no live pressure, no extra push notifications.

Files: design-draft.md (architect draft), critiques.json (compliance and engineering critics), research-by-sport.json (feeds, terms and data APIs per sport), site-map.md (this repo mapped). The app and desk maps are kept off this public repo.
