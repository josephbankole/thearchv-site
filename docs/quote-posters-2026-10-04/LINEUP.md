## Left out, and why

- **"And Solskjaer has won it!"**: the busiest of the ten (at least six listings) and it's already
  the headline of our Barcelona 99 poster.
- **"Knocking Liverpool off their perch"**: the original has an expletive, the clean version is an
  edit, and Liverpool have won the league twice since (2019-20, 2024-25), which invites the reply.
- **"Lads, it's Tottenham"**: it's Roy Keane's anecdote rather than a recorded team talk, and Spurs
  sold their own "Lads, it's Tottenham" clothing after beating Manchester United in the 2025 Europa
  League final.
- **"Football. Bloody hell."**: the founder's call.

## The buyer evidence (search results only)

Nothing in this section was seen on a live listing page. Etsy, Redbubble, Amazon and eBay pages were
blocked to both research passes, so every listing, price and count comes from search results, and no
verbatim buyer review of a quote poster was found. From those results:

- **Buy:** gifting. Sellers title these products "gift for dad", "man cave", "shed", "home bar", and
  the strongest kids' signal is a personalised Manchester United boys' print showing "In 7 carts".
  Commentary Charts (handwritten commentary prints on Not On The High Street) is the one proven
  business near any of these lines.
- **Don't buy:** thin visible demand on the existing quote listings ("no reviews yet", 3 to 4
  favourites, 34 at best). The market splits into £2 to £3 plain downloads and illustrated
  independents at £21 and up, with little well-set physical type in between, which is the gap these
  fill. Complaints about football wall art are about posting damage, late delivery and colours
  printing dark, not about designs. Profanity, rival-baiting and lines that need explaining narrow
  the audience, which is why they're out.

## Risks, named

- **Club name in titles and tags.** Manchester United had a couple's Etsy listings removed in 2018
  and cited the word mark, not only crests. The founder chose searchable names knowing that
  (D-2026-09-25b). The listings stay crest-free, kit-free and face-free and carry the disclaimer.
- **Quoted words.** Spoken words become a literary work once recorded (CDPA 1988 s.3), and short
  extracts can be protected (Infopaq). The founder accepted quote risk for the 21 match posters; the
  same exposure applies here. Commentary (poster 1) is ITV's broadcast.
- **Theatre of Dreams.** Held, as above.
